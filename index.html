A world is waking up

  1. Pick a power from the tray, then tap or drag on the map. Try placing a people, then blessing them.
  2. Zoom with the scroll wheel or a pinch. Move with the hand tool, a right-drag or W A S D.
  3. Tap anyone, from a shepherd to a great hall, to read their story. Big events are written quietly into the Chronicle.

How to play

On a phone, tap, drag and pinch do the same.

Reading the map

Each kingdom's land is tinted in its colour. Its banner shows its name, its people and, after the sword, its soldiers.

Red arrows point from an attacker to the kingdom it is at war with.

Zoomed far out, the Inspect tool opens the codex of the kingdom you tap.

Keeping a world

The World button saves this world to a file you can load later, and shows its seed. Share a seed and others get the same map.
